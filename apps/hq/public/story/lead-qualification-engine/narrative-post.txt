An enterprise buyer with a $20,000 budget lands on a company's website.
They fill out the contact form:
"We need to implement this before the quarter ends. Need a demo ASAP."

Their reward?
"Thanks for reaching out! A representative will get back to you within 2-3 business days."

Meanwhile, an intern asking for free homework advice books a 30-minute discovery call directly on the founder's public calendar for tomorrow at 10 AM.

This is why sales teams complain that "inbound leads are bad."
The leads aren't bad. The routing is broken.

🎯 I built the Lead Qualification Engine:
A multi-variable qualification system that evaluates incoming inquiries in real time across 4 commercial pillars:
1. Intent Clarity (Quote request vs info request)
2. Budget Fit (Approved budget vs $0/free)
3. Urgency (This week vs no rush)
4. ICP Authority (Decision maker vs non-buyer)

How it operates:
⚡ Tier A (80+ score): Instantly unlocks direct VIP calendar booking with the Founder / Senior AE.
🟢 Tier B (60-79 score): Routed to junior SDR team for scheduled discovery.
🟡 Tier C (40-59 score): Sent an automated 3-minute product walkthrough.
🔴 Tier D (<40 score): Sent a polite pricing baseline and FAQ, protecting rep calendars from tire-kickers.

Result: Top buyers book meetings in under 30 seconds. Sales reps spend 100% of their time talking to qualified pipeline.
